# Blurt Support

**Blurt — Campfire Word Game** — a talking game for a group, with no signal
required.

## Contact

Email **[dan@boulderhopkins.com](mailto:dan@boulderhopkins.com)** and I'll get
back to you. Including your iOS version and what you were doing when something
went wrong makes it much easier to fix.

## Common questions

### How do I play?

Pick a mode, pick a category, and tap **Start round**. One person holds the
phone; everyone else plays along.

In **I describe**, you see the word and talk around it without saying it. In
**I guess**, you hold the phone facing out and never see the word, while the
group describes it to you. In **I act**, you act it out without talking.

### In guess mode, how do I score?

You cannot see the buttons, so there are none to aim at. Tap **anywhere** on
the screen for a point. Tap the **dark bar along the bottom** to pass. Each tap
flashes a full-screen "Got it" or "Pass", so the room knows what happened.

### Does it need internet?

No. Every word ships inside the app. Blurt works in airplane mode, in a canyon,
and at a campsite with no bars.

### What is the Everything category?

Every word for that mode, in one pile. It is the default, so a round starts
without anyone having to choose a theme.

### Do the same words come up every time?

No. Each round is dealt shuffled, including a second round of the same
category.

### Can a child who cannot read play?

Yes. Turn on the **Kids** switch. The decks change to 628 simple words across
13 categories, and every one shows a picture. The picture is always the thing
itself, so a child plays by looking.

### What is the "Kid word" button during a round?

It pulls one easy word, with its picture, for whoever just got the phone. The
next word goes back to the grown-up deck. It works in every mode, so nobody has
to sit out a round.

### Can I change category in the middle of a round?

Yes. Tap the category name at the top left. Your score and the timer carry
over.

### Is there a timer?

Only if you want one. It is off by default; turn it on from the setup screen
and pick 30, 60 or 90 seconds. With it off, the round ends when you say so.

### Why does the word get bigger when I turn the phone?

Landscape gives the word the full width, so it reads across a fire or with the
phone propped on a log. Both orientations work in every mode.

### Can I swipe instead of tapping the buttons?

Yes. Drag the word card right to score it, left to skip it.

### A word is wrong, boring, or unfair. Can you change it?

Please tell me. Email the address above with the word and the category. The
same goes for a kid picture that does not obviously mean its word.

### Is there an iPad or Mac version?

Not yet. Blurt is an iPhone app today.

## Reporting a bug

Email the address above. Tell me which mode and category you were in, and what
the word was.
