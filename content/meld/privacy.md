**Meld: Card Score Keeper**
Last updated: 11 August 2026

## The short version

Meld does not collect anything. There are no accounts, no analytics, no
tracking, no advertising, and no servers. Everything you enter stays on your
device.

## What Meld stores

Meld saves the things you type into it so your games are still there when you
come back:

- Player names and the avatar colour you pick for each
- Games, teams, rounds, scores, column layouts, and the notes you pin to a game
- Your saved presets and app settings

This is written to the app's private storage inside your device's Application
Support directory. It is not readable by other apps.

## Where it goes

Nowhere. Meld contains no networking code — it never contacts a server, because
there is no server to contact. Nothing you enter is transmitted, uploaded,
shared, or sold.

If you have iCloud or iTunes device backups turned on, iOS may include Meld's
data in your own encrypted backup, as it does for other apps. That backup is
between you and Apple; Meld is not involved and cannot read it.

## What Meld does not do

- No analytics or crash reporting SDKs
- No advertising identifiers and no ad networks
- No tracking across apps or websites
- No account, sign-in, or email address
- No location, contacts, photos, camera, or microphone access
- No third-party SDKs of any kind

## Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes everything Meld has stored. There is no copy anywhere
else, so there is nothing else to delete and no request to make. Individual
players and games can also be removed inside the app at any time.

## Children

Meld is a score pad for card and board games. It collects no personal
information from anyone, of any age.
