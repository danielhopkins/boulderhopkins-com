**Meld: Card Score Keeper** — a score pad for the card and board games you
already play.

## Contact

Email **[dan@boulderhopkins.com](mailto:dan@boulderhopkins.com)** and I'll get
back to you. Including your iOS version and what you were doing when something
went wrong makes it much easier to fix.

## Common questions

### Where is my data kept?

On your device, and only on your device. Meld has no account and no server —
see the [privacy policy](/meld/privacy/).

### Can I change a game after it has started?

Yes, that's the point. Open **Game settings** from the scoreboard to rename the
game, change the target score, switch between high-score-wins and
low-score-wins, or add and remove columns mid-game. Turning a column off hides
its data but keeps it — turn it back on and the history returns.

### Can I fix a score I entered wrong?

Yes. Tap the cell you want to change and correct it. Past rounds stay editable.

### Can I swap players partway through?

Open **Manage players** from the scoreboard. You can substitute players,
reorder seats, and remove people; past rounds stay intact.

### Does it handle teams?

Yes — set the format to teams during setup, and score by team while keeping
each player's individual record.

### What are presets?

A saved layout — format, columns, and target — that pre-fills a new game.
Meld ships with presets for several classics, and you can save your current
layout as a new one from Game settings.

### What's the "?" button?

A built-in reference of classic card games — how to play and how to score them.
You can import one to turn it into a preset.

### Is there an iPad or Mac version?

Not yet. Meld is an iPhone app today.
